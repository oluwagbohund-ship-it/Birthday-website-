From my heart to you

A message from
Mr. David.

Not a classroom lesson this time. Just a few words I wanted every one of you to hear.

Dear students

Please read this
with your heart.

My dear students,

I have been thinking about the beautiful moments we shared, and I realised that a simple “thank you” may not be enough to express how much your kindness means to me.

Your smiles, your excitement, your thoughtful gifts, and the effort you made to celebrate me made my day truly special. It was not just about the gifts or the pictures. It was about the love, respect, and thoughtfulness behind them.

Being your teacher has given me moments I will always remember. You have reminded me that teaching is not only about lessons, books, and examinations. It is also about connection, encouragement, patience, and the little ways we make one another feel valued.

I want each of you to know that you are important. Keep asking questions. Keep learning. Keep believing that your future can be bright. Do not allow a difficult day, a poor result, or a temporary setback to convince you that you cannot succeed. Growth takes time, and every honest effort counts.

As you move forward, be respectful, be courageous, help one another, and never stop improving. I may not remember every lesson I taught, but I will certainly remember the laughter, the energy, and the memories we created together.

Thank you for making me feel appreciated. Thank you for the love. And thank you for giving me memories worth keeping.

May you all continue to grow in wisdom, confidence, and excellence. I am proud of you, and I am cheering you on.

With gratitude,

Mr. DavidYour teacher · Dave Timi
