A birthday message from Mr. David

There are things
I want you to know.

This time, there’s no lesson to prepare for and no exercise to mark. Just a few words from me to you.

To my dear students

Thank you for making me feel so remembered.

My dear students,

I have been thinking about the way you celebrated me, and I keep coming back to one thing: you made me feel seen. The gifts were thoughtful, the wishes were lovely, but what touched me most was knowing that you had put your hearts into making the day special.

Thank you for your kindness, your excitement and the little gestures that made me laugh and smile. I will remember not only what you gave, but the care you put into giving it.

Teaching you has never been only about lessons and books. It is also about the questions you ask, the ways you encourage one another, the laughter that finds its way into an ordinary day and the many different personalities that make our class what it is. Each of you brings something your own. I hope you never feel you have to be exactly like someone else to matter.

As you keep learning, please be patient with yourselves. Some things take time to understand; some plans change along the way. Keep asking questions, keep trying after a difficult day and be generous with the people around you. Your effort, your character and the way you treat others will take you far.

I am grateful that I got to share these moments with you. Years from now, I may forget the details of a lesson, but I know I will remember your thoughtfulness and the happiness we shared.

Thank you for the gifts, the wishes and, most of all, the kindness behind them. I will carry it with me.

Keep growing. Keep believing in what you can become. I am proud of you, and I will always be cheering you on.

With affection and gratitude,
Mr. David
