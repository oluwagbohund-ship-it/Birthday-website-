My birthday, made brighter by you

Thank you.
Truly, from my heart.

A final note

Dear students,

Thank you for the celebration, the gifts, the kind words and all the small things you did to make me feel appreciated. I noticed the effort, and it meant a great deal to me.

I hope you carry that same kindness with you as you grow. Be curious about the world, be brave enough to keep learning and be the person who helps someone else feel seen. Your future is yours to shape, one thoughtful choice and one determined step at a time.

Wherever you go next, I hope these photographs bring back a little of the happiness we shared. I will remember your generosity with a grateful heart.

You made a memory with me.
I’ll keep it for a long time.

With all my thanks,
Mr. David
